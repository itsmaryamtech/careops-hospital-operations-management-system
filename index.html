How It Works

From scattered records to a clear decision

Detection begins as transparent, rule-based checks. Predictive models are a future capability, offered only once validated on real data.

1Connect data
Stock, beds, equipment and billing records come in.
2Apply your rules

3Alert the right person
Severity, owner and deadline are assigned.
4Act and record
Corrective action logged, results reported.
